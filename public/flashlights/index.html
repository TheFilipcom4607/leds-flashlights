How “Recommended” is ranked

Editor's picks come first and are labelled: five all-round lights chosen by hand (Emisar D4KC and D4V2, Convoy S21E, FireflyLite X4 Stellar, Wurkkos TS26) and a few more for each quick-start goal. Everything else is ranked by points. Points for: LEDs the Emitter Index rates as staples or top picks (+2), a 90+ CRI option (+1), a choice of LEDs (up to +1), Anduril firmware (+1), built-in USB charging and submersible waterproofing (+0.5 each), and complete specs, meaning price, output, throw and weight all stated (up to +1). Lights built for one job (throwers, floods, searchlights, headlamps, dive, laser/LEP, UV and bike lights, plus any with a UV/IR/coloured LED) and oversize lights (head wider than 38 mm or over 250 g) lose 1.5, so the general list isn't full of specialists; the quick-start buttons find them. Mule (no-optic) builds, limited and special-metal editions of a model that also comes in aluminium, and colour/UV-only lights score lower, so the standard version represents the model. Each earlier light from the same brand costs 0.4 points, and each earlier version of the same model 0.5, so no single store or model fills the top. Ties go to the cheaper light. Nobody pays for placement.
