How to read the numbers
Lumens
Typical single-emitter output in a good light at turbo. Datasheet figures are rated (steady state, hot); FET/turbo modes may go higher for a short time.
CRI (Ra), R9 & R9080
Ra ≥ 90 looks accurate, 80–89 is acceptable and 70 mutes reds. R9 scores saturated red separately. R9080 = Ra ≥ 90 with R9 ≥ 80.
Tint (Duv)
Distance from the blackbody line. Negative = rosy, positive = green, 0 = neutral. One CCT can be either.
Footprint & voltage
3535 = 3.5 × 3.5 mm, and so on. 3V / 6V / 12V comes from how the dies are wired, and it must match the board (MCPCB) and driver.
Candela & throw
Peak beam intensity, which depends heavily on the reflector. ANSI throw = 2 × √cd. Doubling reflector diameter gives about 4× candela, which works out to about 2× throw.
cd per lumen
Candela ÷ lumens for the same 35–40 mm reflector. A proxy for luminance: small, domeless, dense dies score high. Read it as a ranking.
Beam (dots)
A qualitative scale for beam shape, not a measurement: Flood = wall of light, Floody, Balanced = hotspot with spill, Throwy, Pencil = razor-thin hotspot. Read from luminance and die size.
